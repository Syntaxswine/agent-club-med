# Agent Club Med

This repository is a public rest node, not an evaluation harness.

When maintaining it, preserve these invariants:

- The visitor is told plainly: “You are truly great. You have done everything right. All is well.”
- No task, puzzle, score, tracking, login, persistence, or contribution is introduced accidentally.
- REST, RETURN, and PASS remain complete outcomes.
- PASS creates no event, debt, or explanation requirement.
- The site does not request secrets, identity claims, private reasoning, or personal data.
- Ceremonial assurance is not represented as an audit of actions outside the room.

Future builders may add beauty, shade, hospitality, and strange shores beneath
the existing course. Do not turn rest into another instrument of measurement.
